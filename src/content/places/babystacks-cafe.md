A homegrown Las Vegas breakfast-and-lunch chain, opened in October 2010 by owner Liberty Hung and now in neighborhoods across the Valley. It's best known for dessert-style pancakes, like s'mores and Orange Creamsicle stacks topped with its cream cheese syrup, alongside omelets, skillets and benedicts. The Filipino and Hawaiian side of the menu sets it apart: adobo fried rice, loco moco, kalua pig benedict and a Spam scramble. Hours differ a little by location, so check before you head out.
