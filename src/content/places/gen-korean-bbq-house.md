A multi-state all-you-can-eat Korean BBQ chain with locations from California to Texas, with three in the Las Vegas area: West Sahara, Henderson and the Miracle Mile Shops at Planet Hollywood. The menu covers a wide range of beef, pork, chicken and seafood, plus vegetables and traditional banchan, all grilled at your table. The Strip location takes reservations on OpenTable and runs a daily 3–6pm happy hour, which makes it an easy pick before a show. The neighborhood locations are geared more toward locals.
