A Southern California tea-and-coffee chain with 43 stores in five states, including three in Las Vegas: Spring Mountain in Chinatown and two in the southwest Valley. The menu centers on house-brewed teas, milk teas and coffee. Its signatures include the 7 Leaves House Coffee, the Jasmine Sea Cream Tea, Japanese matcha drinks and a Strawberry Hibiscus Refresher, plus fresh baked goods. Taro milk tea gets a lot of love from regulars. The Spring Mountain location is open until 10pm daily, which makes it a good after-dinner stop on the Chinatown strip.
