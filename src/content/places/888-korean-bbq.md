One of the best-known all-you-can-eat Korean BBQ spots in Chinatown, set in a strip-mall plaza on Spring Mountain Road. The AYCE menu runs to more than 50 items, and you cook at in-table grills. It's open until midnight every day and gets busy on weekend nights, so go early or expect a wait. Plenty of bakeries and Asian markets nearby help pass the time. If you'd rather have Japanese-style grilling, the owners also run 888 Japanese BBQ on Decatur.
